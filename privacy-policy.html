Sightline

Privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · Applies to the Sightline iPhone app

Sightline collects no data. Everything you save stays on your iPhone.

  • No account or sign-up
  • No analytics, ads or tracking
  • No Sightline servers: we never receive your routes, places or location

What Sightline keeps on your phone

To work, the app stores these things in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • Routes you plan and save, and your ratings of them
  • Places you save, such as cafés, viewpoints and great roads
  • Corner reviews and hazard notes you add
  • Spots you mark during a ride, with the time, position and speed at that moment
  • Photos you choose to attach
  • Your settings, such as phone mount position and map dimming

None of this is sent to us or anyone else by Sightline.

Location

Sightline asks for your location only while you are using the app. It never asks for "Always" access and does not track you in the background.

Your location is used on the phone to start a route from where you are, follow your progress along a route, show the corners and hazards ahead, and record the position of a spot you mark. You can change or turn off location access at any time in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Sightline.

Maps, search and route planning

Sightline uses Apple's built-in map services (MapKit) to show maps, search for places and plan routes. When you do this, your iPhone sends the request (for example the text you search for, the stops on your route and the area shown on the map) directly to Apple, which handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy. Sightline does not receive a copy.

If you use Siri or the Action button to mark a spot, that request is handled by Apple in the same way.

Photos

Sightline only sees the photos you pick with the iPhone's photo picker. It does not have access to the rest of your photo library. A copy of each chosen photo is saved with the review or place it belongs to.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Sightline's data is included in that backup like any other app's. Apple's terms apply to those backups.

Deleting your data

Swipe to delete any route, place or marked spot in the app. Deleting the Sightline app removes everything it stored.

Children

Sightline is made for riders and drivers and is not aimed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of Sightline offers features that need data to leave your phone, such as syncing between devices, they will be optional and explained before you turn them on.

Common questions

  • The map doesn't show where I am. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Sightline is set to "While Using the App".
  • How do I save battery on a long ride? Unplugged, the ride map moves in steps instead of gliding, which saves power. The screen and GPS still use battery, so on long rides a phone charger on the bike makes the biggest difference.
  • Are the corner colours and hazard warnings always right? No. Everything in Sightline is indicative. Always check the road for yourself, ride to the conditions and follow road signs and the road rules.
